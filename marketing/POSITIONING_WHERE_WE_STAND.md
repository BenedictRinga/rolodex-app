# LoopKeeper — Where we stand (positioning note)

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Use:** App discourse (About page), App Store/Play listings, shares, investor conversations. Plain commercial language, not academic.

---

## The bet

The industry is racing to **replace human agency** — build the agent that does it all, so the person can
stop showing up to their own life. We chose the opposite road, deliberately:

> **Go where agency is low — not missing — and reinforce it. The person stays the agent; the AI carries the friction.**

## Why LoopKeeper is the right shape for this

People rarely stop **caring**. They stop **starting**. The reply, the check-in, the promise, the birthday —
the intention is fully present; the act of beginning is what's blocked. That gap is universal, and the
market misreads it as a discipline problem when it is a **friction problem**.

LoopKeeper is built exactly there:

- It raises the **person you owe** — not a chore list of everyone.
- It writes **the first sentence, in your voice** — then hands it straight back.
- **You are always the sender.** It never sends, calls, or pretends to be you.
- It lives in the app the person is already in (**WhatsApp**) — no new network to build, no new habit to learn.
- The metric is **loops closed** — real follow-through — not contacts stored.

## The benefit (why it's good for people, not just users)

- **More human agency, not less.** The world automates people out of their own relationships and reputations;
  this hands the life back.
- **Your words stay yours.** Nothing is farmed out. You remain the author of your relationships.
- **Nothing to perform.** Not a social network, not a feed, not a place to be seen. Three live loops; the rest wait.
- **Private by construction.** Device-first; the address book stays on the device.

## Commercially, why it holds

"Do it for you" tools are commoditised and quietly resented. "Help you do it yourself" earns trust,
retention, and word of mouth — and trust compounds where autopilot does not. LoopKeeper wins on an honest
health metric (**loops closed**), which is the thing that keeps people, and it needs no network effect to
be useful to the first user.
